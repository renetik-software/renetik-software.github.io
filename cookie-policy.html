Cookie Policy

Last updated: 7 August 2026

This policy explains how renetik.com uses cookies and browser storage, and how you control it. It applies together with the Privacy Policy.

Controller and contact

René Doháň is responsible for the site's data processing. For cookie or privacy questions, email dohan.rene@gmail.com.

Scope

This policy covers all of renetik.com, including the Music Apps pages under /app/. They share one origin, so a single consent choice applies across both.

Consent

renetik.com separates storage into two categories:

  • Essential storage works without any consent and is needed for the site to function. It is set the first time you visit.
  • Analytics cookies are non-essential. They are set only after you consent through the banner, and consent can be withdrawn at any time.

When you first visit, a banner asks whether you accept analytics cookies. Choosing Reject means no analytics cookies are set. Your choice is remembered for up to 12 months and applies to both / and /app/.

Withdrawing consent

You can withdraw your analytics consent at any time. Withdrawing stops analytics cookies from being set on later visits and is as easy as giving consent.

You can also reopen the consent banner on any page from the footer “Manage cookies” link.

External resources

The site loads fonts and icons from Google Fonts and Font Awesome, and project pages can embed videos. Loading these resources shares ordinary request information — such as your IP address and browser — with the relevant provider. YouTube embeds use the privacy-enhanced youtube-nocookie.com domain.

Your rights

Depending on the law that applies to you, you have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict, port, or object to the processing of your data; to withdraw consent without affecting earlier lawful processing; and to complain to a supervisory authority. For renetik.com that authority is the Úrad na ochranu osobných údajov SR (ÚOOÚ), https://uoou.gov.sk. Email dohan.rene@gmail.com to make a request.

Changes

This policy may be updated when the site's cookies or storage change. The date above identifies the current version.
