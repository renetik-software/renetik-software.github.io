Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 August 2026

This policy explains how René Doháň's software portal at renetik.com handles information. The portal has no account registration, contact form, newsletter, advertising, online payment, or user-generated content.

This policy covers all of renetik.com, including the Music Apps pages under /app/. They share one origin and one set of cookies and storage, described here and in full in the Cookie Policy.

Controller and contact

René Doháň is responsible for the portal's data processing. For privacy questions or requests, email dohan.rene@gmail.com.

Information handled when you visit

The hosting and content-delivery infrastructure may process standard request data needed to deliver and secure the site, such as an IP address, request time, requested page, browser or user-agent information, and error or security events. This processing supports reliable and secure delivery of the portal and is based on legitimate interests in operating and protecting the site.

Google Analytics

The portal uses Google Analytics to understand aggregate use of its pages, but only after you consent. Analytics does not load and no Analytics cookie is set until you accept the cookie banner; choosing Reject, or leaving the banner without a choice, keeps it off. The default Google Analytics implementation can collect page and session activity, approximate location, and browser and device information, and can use a first-party _ga identifier.

You can grant, refuse, or withdraw this consent at any time from the cookie banner or the footer's "Manage cookies" link, and you can still limit or clear Analytics cookies through your browser settings. See the Cookie Policy for the full list of cookies and storage this involves.

Google acts as an analytics service provider and may process data outside your country under its applicable contractual and transfer safeguards. See Google's Analytics privacy and data safeguards and default data collection.

Essential browser storage

The portal uses browser storage for functional preferences that need no consent: your chosen language (locale), a temporary deep-link recovery value used when a static host redirects a portal URL (servicesRedirect), and your cookie-consent choice and when you made it (cookieConsent). These values are used only to provide the requested site behavior. You can clear them through your browser settings; clearing cookieConsent simply means the banner asks again.

External resources and embedded media

The site loads fonts and icons from Google Fonts and Font Awesome. Project pages can embed videos using YouTube's privacy-enhanced youtube-nocookie.com domain. Loading those resources shares ordinary request information, including your IP address and browser information, with the relevant provider. Playing or interacting with a video can allow YouTube to process additional usage information under its own policy.

Links to LinkedIn, GitHub, Stack Overflow, WhatsApp, social networks, app stores, client sites, and other external services do not send those services information merely because the link is displayed. Once you follow a link, the destination's privacy policy applies.

Direct contact

Email, telephone, WhatsApp, and profile links open an external application or service; the portal itself does not submit a contact form. If you contact René, the information you choose to provide is used to respond, discuss an opportunity, maintain relevant correspondence, and, where applicable, take steps toward or perform an agreement. Correspondence is retained only for as long as it remains relevant to those purposes or is required for legal obligations.

Retention and recipients

Functional preferences remain in your browser until you change or clear them. Analytics retention is controlled in the Google Analytics property. Hosting providers, Google, embedded media providers, and communication services receive information only for the purposes described above. Personal information is not sold.

Your rights

Depending on the law that applies to you, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability, or objection; to withdraw consent without affecting earlier lawful processing; and to complain to a supervisory authority. For renetik.com that authority is the Úrad na ochranu osobných údajov SR (ÚOOÚ), https://uoou.gov.sk. Email dohan.rene@gmail.com to make a request.

Changes

This policy may be updated when the portal's services or data flows change. The date above identifies the current version.
